Welcome to the **Grains of Time Website Manager**! 

This guide is written specifically for members, social media managers, officers, and future alumni who manage the Grains of Time website. **You do not need any coding experience, technical knowledge, or developer software.** Managing this website is designed to be as simple and intuitive as using Instagram or Squarespace.

## 1. How to Log In

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge).
2. Go to: **`https://grainsoftime.org/studio`**  
   *(If testing on a computer locally before launch, use `http://localhost:3000/studio`)*
3. You will see a login prompt. Click **"Log in with Google"** or your authorized account.
4. Once authenticated, you will be taken directly into your visual dashboard.

> **Tip**: Bookmark `https://grainsoftime.org/studio` in your browser favorites so you can quickly get to it whenever you need to make an update!

## 2. Tour of Your Website Manager

On the left side of your screen is the navigation menu. Everything is organized into clear, plain-language sections:

- 🏠 **Homepage** — Edit the hero image, main title, intro paragraphs, and quick links.
- 📖 **About Us** — Edit our history story, rehearsal traditions, and core tenets.
- 👥 **Members & Alumni** — Add new members, change vocal sections, upload portraits, and move graduating seniors to Alumni.
- 📅 **Concerts & Events** — Add upcoming shows, post ticket links, announce auditions, or mark events as Sold Out.
- 🖼️ **Photo Gallery** — Upload performance photos, tag them by category, and credit photographers.
- 🎵 **Repertoire (Our Songs)** — Update current concert setlists, student arrangers, and listening links.
- 💿 **Music & Recordings** — Post albums, tracklists, and Spotify/Apple Music links.
- 🕒 **Our History (Timeline)** — Add historical milestones, photos, and memories dating back to 1968.
- 📬 **Booking Requests** — View inquiries submitted through the website and track contact status.
- ⚙️ **Website Settings** — Update the announcement banner, social media links (Instagram, Spotify, Merch store), and contact emails.

At the very top of your screen, click **"↗ View Live Website"** anytime to open the public site in a new tab.

## 3. Editing the Homepage

To change what appears on the front page of the site:

1. Click **Homepage** in the left menu.
2. The page is divided into 4 simple tabs:
   - **Hero Section**:
     - **Hero Image**: Click **"Upload"** to replace the large cover photo. Click the crop icon (✂) to select the focal point so faces don't get cut off on mobile phones.
     - **Main Heading**: Change the large headline (default: `Grains of Time`).
     - **Subtitle**: Change the sub-line (e.g. `NC State's Premier All-Male A Cappella Ensemble`).
     - **Short Description**: Change the poetic summary line.
     - **About Snippet**: Change the introductory paragraph.
   - **Introduction**:
     - **Pull Quote**: The large featured quotation on the page.
     - **Quote Attribution**: Who said the quote.
   - **Quick Links**:
     - Edit the 4 highlight cards that lead visitors into different sections of the website.
   - **Featured Content**:
     - **Show Upcoming Event on Homepage**: Turn this switch ON or OFF.
     - **Number of Gallery Photos**: Set how many photos appear in the homepage photo strip (usually 6).
     - **Button Texts & Links**: Change where the two main buttons at the top point to.
3. When you are finished, click the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.

## 5. Managing Members & Roster

> **Automatic Roster Sync**: If opening the Studio for the first time or setting up a fresh database, the top navigation bar includes an automated roster sync indicator. If no active members exist in Sanity, it automatically seeds all 15 active member records and uploads their official headshots. You can also click **↺ Re-sync Roster** in the top bar at any time to re-populate or update the member list.

### Adding a New Member
1. In the left menu, click **Members & Alumni** → **Current Active Members**.
2. Click the **+ Create** button in the upper right.
3. Fill in their details:
   - **Full Name**: e.g., *Marcus Rivera*
   - **URL Name**: Click **"Generate"** — this creates the web link automatically.
   - **Member Status**: Leave set to **Current Member**.
   - **Vocal Part**: Select *Tenor 1*, *Tenor 2*, *Baritone*, *Bass*, or *Vocal Percussion*.
   - **Sort Position**: Enter a number (e.g., `10`, `20`, `30`). Lower numbers appear first on the page.
   - **Leadership Role (optional)**: e.g., *President*, *Music Director*, *Business Manager*.
   - **Portrait Photo**: Upload a high-quality photo. **Crucial**: Click the crop icon (✂) and drag the circle onto the member's face.
   - **Graduation Year**, **Major**, **Hometown**, **Short Bio**, **Fun Fact**, and **Favorite Song**.
4. Click **Publish**.

### Moving a Member to Alumni Status
When a member graduates:
1. Find the member in the list and click on their name.
2. Under **Member Status**, change the selection from **Current Member** to **Alumni / Former Member**.
3. Make sure their **Graduation Year** is filled in.
4. Click **Publish**.  
   *They will instantly move from the active roster into the Alumni Archive tab automatically.*

## 6. Managing Concerts & Events

### Announcing an Upcoming Show
1. Click **Concerts & Events** → **Upcoming & Live Events**.
2. Click **+ Create**.
3. Enter:
   - **Event Name**: e.g., *Spring Finale Showcase 2026*
   - **URL Name**: Click **"Generate"**.
   - **Event Type**: Choose *Concert*, *Campus Event*, *Private*, or *Audition*.
   - **Event Status**: Select **Live — visible on the website**.
   - **Feature on Homepage**: Turn this switch ON if this is the next major upcoming show you want highlighted on the home page.
   - **Start Date & Time**: Pick the exact date and curtain time.
   - **Venue Name & Address**: e.g., *Stewart Theatre, Talley Student Union*.
   - **Short Description**: 1–2 sentences for social cards and previews.
   - **Ticket / RSVP Link**: Paste the ticket sales link (e.g., Ticketmaster, university box office).
   - **Free Admission**: Toggle ON if there are no tickets required.
   - **Event Poster**: Upload the promotional flyer or graphics.
4. Click **Publish**.

### Marking an Event as Sold Out or Cancelled
- If a concert sells out, open the event, switch the status to **Sold Out**, and click **Publish**. A red badge will appear on the site.
- If weather forces a cancellation, switch status to **Cancelled** and click **Publish**.

### Archiving a Past Show
After a concert finishes:
1. Open the event.
2. Change status to **Past Event — moved to archive**.
3. (Optional) Paste in the songs you performed under **Concert Setlist** or write a short **Event Recap**.
4. Click **Publish**.

## 7. Adding & Organizing Photos (Gallery)

1. Click **Photo Gallery** in the left menu.
2. Click **+ Create**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Photo Title**: e.g., *Spring Showcase Stewart Theatre*
   - **Photo**: Upload the JPEG or PNG file.
   - **Describe This Photo**: Brief description for accessibility (e.g., *Grains of Time tenor section singing live*).
   - **Caption**: Optional story or detail shown beneath the photo.
   - **Photo Credit**: Name of photographer or student media (e.g., *Photo by Technician*).
   - **Category**: Check *Live Concerts*, *Rehearsals*, *Tours & Travel*, or *Backstage*.
   - **Show on Homepage**: Check this box if you want this photo to appear in the homepage carousel.
4. Click **Publish**.

## 8. Managing Our Repertoire (Songs)

1. Click **Repertoire (Our Songs)** → **Current Concert Setlist**.
2. Click **+ Create**.
3. Enter:
   - **Song Title**: e.g., *Superstition*
   - **Original Artist**: e.g., *Stevie Wonder*
   - **Arranger**: Name of student arranger or alumnus (e.g., *Arr. by John Doe '24*).
   - **Currently Performing?**: Select **Yes — in our current setlist**.
   - **Genre**: Contemporary Pop, Classic Rock, Soul & R&B, NC State Tradition, etc.
   - **Listening Link (optional)**: Paste a YouTube link of the group performing it.
4. Click **Publish**.

When retiring a song for the season, simply open it, switch **Currently Performing?** to **No — a past arrangement**, and hit **Publish**. It moves seamlessly to the past repertoire tab.

## 11. Reviewing Booking Requests

When clients or organizations fill out the **/book** form on the public website:
1. In the left menu, click **Booking Requests** → **New / Unread Requests**.
2. Click any inquiry to see:
   - Client name, email, phone number, and organization.
   - Requested date, venue location, gig duration, and performance details.
3. After you email or call the client, change the **Status Workflow** from **● New / Unread** to **◐ In Communication** or **✓ Confirmed Booking**.
4. Click **Publish** to save your status update.

## 13. Drafting, Previewing, and Publishing Changes

Every change you make in the Website Manager goes through a safe 3-step lifecycle:

```
[ You Edit Content ] ──> Automatically Saved as DRAFT (Private)
                                   │
                                   ▼
                            [ Preview Site ] (Optional)
                                   │
                                   ▼
                         [ Click PUBLISH ] ──> LIVE ON THE WEBSITE
```

### The Difference Between "Draft" and "Live"
- **Draft**: While you are typing or uploading photos, your work is saved in the dashboard automatically. **Nothing changes on the public website yet.** You can close your laptop and come back tomorrow; your drafts will still be waiting for you.
- **Publish**: When you are happy with your changes, click the green **Publish** button in the bottom right corner. A confirmation will appear:  
  *“Published successfully. Your changes are now live.”*

## 14. How Cropping and Images Work

You **never** need to resize photos in Photoshop or external apps before uploading. The Website Manager handles responsive sizing, mobile optimization, and loading speed automatically.

### The Focal Point Tool (Crop Icon)
When uploading a portrait or event poster:
1. Click on the uploaded image thumbnail.
2. Click the small **Crop icon** (looks like a pair of scissors or a crosshair ✂).
3. A circle indicator will appear over the photo.
4. **Drag the circle over the most important part of the photo** (for example, a person's face or the main title of a poster).
5. No matter what device a visitor uses—an iPhone, an iPad, or a wide desktop monitor—the system will keep that circle in the frame!

## 15. Inviting & Removing Team Members (Offboarding)

At the start or end of each academic school year, access should be updated as officers transition:

### Adding a New Officer/Manager
1. An existing administrator logs into **[sanity.io/manage](https://www.sanity.io/manage)** using their account.
2. Select your **Grains of Time** project.
3. Click the **Members** tab in the top navigation.
4. Click **Invite Member**.
5. Type the new officer's email address and choose their role:
   - **Administrator**: Full permissions (recommended for President, Business Manager, or Webmaster).
   - **Editor**: Can edit members, events, photos, songs, and text, but cannot delete the project or alter database security (recommended for Social Media Managers and general officers).
6. Click **Send Invite**. The incoming officer will receive an email to activate their account.

### Removing a Graduating Senior
1. In **[sanity.io/manage](https://www.sanity.io/manage)** → **Members**.
2. Find the graduating officer's name.
3. Click the three dots menu (**...**) next to their name and select **Remove Member**.
4. Their access is immediately revoked. The public website remains completely untouched.

## 16. Troubleshooting & What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

### "I accidentally typed over something or want to undo my changes"
- At the bottom of any document you are editing, click the **History / Document Activity** icon (looks like a clock with a counter-clockwise arrow).
- You can inspect past edits and revert the document back to any previous version with a single click.

### "I published an update, but I don't see it on the website"
1. Hold down `Shift` and press the `Refresh` button in your browser (Hard Refresh to clear browser cache).
2. Changes typically appear on the live site within a few seconds. If it takes longer, check that you clicked **Publish** (the button will turn from green to grey when there are no uncommitted changes).

### "An image failed to upload"
- Make sure the image file is a standard JPEG, PNG, or WebP format.
- Avoid uploading RAW camera files (e.g. `.CR2` or `.NEF`). Export them as JPEGs first.
